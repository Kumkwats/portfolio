Participe 3.0 – Unity Developer

Context

The Participe 3.0 project aims to accompany young autistic adults with intellectual disability to help them gain autonomy by enabling them to live in shared, inclusive housing. To achieve this, the project develops digital tools such as applications or immersive environments to help the young adults manage their daily lives and prepare them for various daily situations. Those tools are concieved with direct feedback from the concerned young adults, their family and the supervisory staff living with them so that the tools are truly adapted to their needs.

The objective is to foster their independence, strengthen their decision-making capacity and enable them to participate in our society.


Role and contributions

As an intern at the Centre de Recherche en Éducation de Nantes (CREN), I participated in this project by designing a Virtual Reality module using Unity. The aim of this module is to enable the young adults to learn and memorize the steps for cooking food in a microwave within a safe and familiar environment.
